# Matching model derived from the supplied Youth-ID call

The attached example was reviewed visually page by page. It described a 12-month Erasmus+ youth initiative with:

- a two-day expert training in Strasbourg;
- national scaling seminars;
- local mentoring and community-project design;
- an international exchange in Poland and an advocacy event in Brussels;
- a target audience of active youth workers or professionals in youth organisations;
- commitments to EU values, inclusive community planning, training attendance, and youth mentoring;
- covered international mobility costs, a YouthPass certificate, resources, and policy influence.

The form requested name, contact email, organisation/affiliation, residence in Belgium, France, Italy, Poland, Cyprus or North Macedonia, youth-work experience, motivation, and availability.

## How those observations shaped this monitor

The scoring model therefore looks for more than topic words. It separately records evidence for:

1. audience (PhD students, researchers, youth workers and related professionals);
2. organisation type (youth, education and research institutions);
3. opportunity type (short training, exchange, seminar, mobility, fellowship or project);
4. technical interests (cybersecurity, IT, AI and workflow/process automation);
5. youth and education signals;
6. research signals;
7. European geography.

Explicit age caps are rejected by default. If a page explicitly says there is no age limit, the alert marks that positively. If age is not mentioned, the alert says **not stated**; it never presents silence as confirmed eligibility.

The source PDF is intentionally not included in this public repository because it contains application-form and account context that is unnecessary for the software.
